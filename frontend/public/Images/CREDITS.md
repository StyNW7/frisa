# Food photography credits

Every food and recipe photo in FRISA is a real photograph, stored locally so the app works offline and under its `img-src 'self'` CSP.

- `food/*.webp` - 384 px transparent cut-outs. Backgrounds were removed and the subject centred on a square canvas.
- `food/category-*.webp` - still lifes composed from the cut-outs above, used when an item name has no exact photo.
- `recipes/*.webp` - dish photographs, resized and re-encoded.

Sources:

- **TheMealDB** (https://www.themealdb.com): ingredient and meal images, used under its free non-commercial/educational terms.
- **Wikimedia Commons** (https://commons.wikimedia.org): each image is under the licence listed below. CC BY / CC BY-SA images were modified (background removal, cropping, resizing).
